Artemis-II Simulation

2026 Apr 12 · Simulation

A complete N-body Python simulation of NASA's Artemis II free-return lunar flyby trajectory. The project documents the mission setup, optimization decisions, physics model, and generated visual outputs from trajectory exploration. I amd still evaluating this model and learning along the way.

There were many aspects of supposedly simple space-science that we take for granted get questioned to the core. But that's what keeps us excited about the future.

One could try so many exciting things with this simulation. For example, one could compare it with Apollo mission by altering the parking orbit to 185 km. Or removing the Sun's gravitational impact on the model. Or changing the flyby trajectory a little to see that error increases at an astronomical scale.

I find this to be a perfect model for my kids to tinker with and be fascinated with science. There is nothing more exciting!

I am going to add the Artemis-III improvements and see if we can land on the moon!
