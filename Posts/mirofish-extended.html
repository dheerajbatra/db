Mirofish Extended

2026 Apr 12 · LLM Experiments

Extension work around the Mirofish model workflow, focused on practical experimentation and customization for local use. I used the original Mirofish projects and extended that to ingest local documents, website links, uses research resources and latest news to make predictions about a given topic. To extend it further, I used the concept to add Advisor's Panel that simulates debates, leader's insights, brainstorming ideas with top indistry leaders.

GitHub: https://github.com/dheerajbatra/Mirofish-Extended
